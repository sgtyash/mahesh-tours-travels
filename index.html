              Yes! Two clean ISI-certified helmets are included free. On your Honda Activa, you can easily explore Cellular Jail, Corbyn’s Cove Beach, Chidiya Tapu Sunset Point, Wandoor Beach, and Marina Park.
            

          


          

            
            

              Indian nationals do not need a passport or Restricted Area Permit for Port Blair, Havelock, or Neil Island—just a valid government ID. Foreign nationals receive their permit automatically on arrival at Port Blair airport.
            

          


          

            
            

              Airtel, Jio, and BSNL offer strong 4G/5G connectivity in Port Blair and good coverage around Havelock and Neil Island hubs.
            

          


          

            
            

              October to May is ideal with calm turquoise seas, clear underwater visibility for scuba diving and snorkeling, and pleasant coastal breezes. Even during monsoon months (June–September), Port Blair’s lush greenery and scenic drives look magical.
            

          


          

            
            

              High-speed private catamarans (Makruzz, Nautika, Green Ocean) and government ferries connect Haddo Jetty in Port Blair to Swaraj Dweep (90 mins) and Shaheed Dweep (60 mins). We pre-book your preferred ferry seats so you never worry about sold-out slots.
            

          


          

            
            

              UPI (GPay/PhonePe) and cards work smoothly at most hotels, cafes, and fuel stations in Port Blair, Havelock, and Neil Island. Carrying a little cash is helpful for beachside shacks, local coconut stalls, and remote ferry jetties.

              Sunrise happens early around 5:00 AM and sunset is around 5:15–5:30 PM. Most beaches close shortly after sunset for marine conservation and safety, so starting your day early lets you make the most of every island.
